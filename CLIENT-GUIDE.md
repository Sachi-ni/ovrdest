# OVRZD: how to manage your website products

Your website shows the T-shirts from your **Studio** (the admin page). Anything you change there appears on the website within about a minute.

**Your Studio address:**  https://ovrzd-admin.sanity.studio/   **Login email:** ______________________

You can do everything from a phone or a computer.

## The golden rule
After every change, press the green **Publish** button. Changes that are not published do not appear on the website.

## Add a new T-shirt
1. Open the Studio and press the **+** (Create) button, then choose **T-shirt**.
2. Upload the **photo**.
3. Type the **design name**.
4. Choose the **colour**.
5. Type the **price** as numbers only, like `3500`.
6. Tick the **sizes** you have.
7. Leave **In stock** switched on.
8. Set the **display order** if you want it near the top (1 shows first).
9. Press **Publish**.

## Change a price
Open the T-shirt, change the price, press **Publish**.

## Mark something sold out
Open the T-shirt, switch **In stock** off, press **Publish**. The photo goes grey, shows "Sold out", and customers cannot order it. Switch it back on when you have stock again.

## Change a photo
Open the T-shirt, click the photo, upload the new one, press **Publish**.

## Remove a T-shirt
Open it, press the three dots at the top right, choose **Delete**. If you might sell it again, use **In stock off** instead.

## Photo tips
- Use wide (landscape) photos with good light.
- Phone photos are fine. They are resized automatically.

## How customers order
They tap **WhatsApp** or **Email** on a design. A message with the design, size, price and "Cash on delivery" is filled in for them. They send it, and you reply to confirm their name, address and phone number.

## If something looks wrong
- Product missing on the website: check it says **Published**, then reload the page.
- Wrong price showing: reload the page after a minute.
