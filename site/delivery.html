Good to know

Delivery, payment & returns

Everything you need before you order.

Delivery

  • Island-wide delivery by courier.
  • Delivery time and fee depend on your location. We confirm both on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • We message you when your parcel is dispatched.
  • Please give a full address and a phone number the courier can reach.

How payment works

  • 1. Order by tapping WhatsApp or Email on any design.
  • 2. We confirm availability, total price and delivery on WhatsApp.
  • 3. Pay cash on delivery when the parcel reaches you.
  • No online payment is needed and we never ask for card details.

Returns & exchanges

  • Wrong size or a faulty item? Message us on WhatsApp soon after delivery and we will arrange an exchange.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition.
  • Send your order details and a photo so we can sort it quickly.
  • We confirm exchange availability and any delivery cost on WhatsApp.
